How it works: four steps from notes to files

  1. Step 1

    Draft

    Prism turns your source into one layout-free draft, with a source for every number.

    • Notes
    • Transcripts
    • Documents
    • Pasted text
    • Photos

  2. Step 2

    Proof

    Brand and claim checks run first. Then you read it and make your edits.

    The checks flag problems in the draft. The edits are yours.

  3. Step 3

    Approve

    Nothing gets formatted until you sign off on the words.

    Words are settled before layout starts. That keeps every format consistent and makes revisions cheap.

  4. Step 4

    Produce

    Prism builds every format you asked for from the approved draft.

    Each format follows its own layout rules for size, safe areas and logo placement. See the formats

07 · Questions

Before you start

What can I give Prism?

Notes, transcripts, documents, pasted text and photos. Whatever you have is enough to start a draft.

Which formats does Prism build?

One-pagers and sheets, flyers, case studies, trifold brochures, slide decks, blog posts and headers, social posts, stories, carousels, email headers and HTML emails. Prism builds only the formats on that list.

Does Prism replace review or a designer?

No. Prism drafts and builds. You proof the draft, approve the words and read what goes out.

What happens when I change the draft later?

Edits to the draft flow into each format. Your layout choices stay put.

How does Prism keep outputs on brand?

It reads your design system: tokens, components, format rules and voice. Swap the system and every output follows it.

Where do the numbers come from?

Your material. Every number in the draft carries its source, and sample content is labelled as sample.

When should I use quick mode?

When you need one output now. Quick mode builds a single format in one pass with no proof step, so read it before it goes out.
